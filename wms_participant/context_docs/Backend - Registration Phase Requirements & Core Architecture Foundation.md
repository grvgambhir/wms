### Technical Base Requirements and Architectural Foundation: Como Lake 2027 Games Management Software

##### 1\. Document Mission and Strategic Objectives

This architectural baseline mandates the technical North Star for the Como Lake 2027 Games, establishing the immutable requirements for the critical Phase A production launch. It serves as a binding directive to transition raw RFP requirements into a high-availability Architectural Foundation capable of supporting a multi-province international event. The mission is to deliver a fault-tolerant, secure, and configurable "Operations-Ready" ecosystem that mitigates the inherent risks of high-concurrency registration and complex Masters-level sports orchestration.The primary objective is to move from static procurement specifications to a functional service environment. This foundation must support the unique complexities of Masters sport—where age-based eligibility, proficiency grading, and dynamic team structures vary across 40+ disciplines—while ensuring that the system remains stable under the extreme pressure of the 17 September 2026 opening bell.**Primary Deployment Milestones**| Milestone | Target Date | Minimum Outcome || \------ | \------ | \------ || **Phase A Production Launch** | 17 September 2026 | Live, secure participant registration; core rules and payment orchestration. || **Operations-Ready Gate (Phase B)** | 30 September 2026 | Stabilization complete; 15,000 EOI records migrated; waitlists active. || **Competition Readiness Gate** | June 2027 | Final sport configurations and results interfaces formally accepted. || **Operational Readiness Window** | July – August 2027 | Dress rehearsals, load tests, and technical runbook sign-off. || **Games-Time Operation** | 17 – 26 September 2027 | 24/7 command-mode software support across Como, Lecco, and Varese. || **Phase F: Post-Event Closure** | 31 December 2027 | Final financial reconciliation; certified data deletion per Swiss Law. |  
These strategic milestones dictate a foundational design pattern prioritizing horizontal scalability and idempotent transactional logic over bespoke, hard-coded development.

##### 2\. Architectural Scaling and Performance Baselines

Capacity planning and concurrency management are mission-critical architectural constraints. In the Masters Games context, popular sports frequently reach capacity within minutes of launch; any failure in quota management or system responsiveness would cause irreversible reputational damage and "overselling" race conditions. To satisfy requirement  **NFR-01** , the solution must utilize a multi-tier, fault-tolerant hosted architecture with strictly isolated production and non-production environments to ensure the integrity of the delivery roadmap.The system design envelope must accommodate a base of 14,000 paid athletes with a proven ceiling of 20,000 to prevent re-platforming. The database architecture must manage a unified persona layer of 50,000 records (athletes, workforce, and stakeholders) through sophisticated indexing and horizontal scaling. Non-negotiable "Concurrency and Burst Management" targets include 2,000 concurrent authenticated sessions and a 5x transaction burst capacity.**System Performance & Service Level Targets**| Measure | Target (95th Percentile) | Target (99th Percentile) | Basis for Measurement || \------ | \------ | \------ | \------ || **Participant Page Response** | ≤ 2.5 seconds | ≤ 5.0 seconds | Authenticated pages at service edge under peak. || **Administration Searches** | ≤ 3.0 seconds | N/A | Standard view/save operations in admin portal. || **Transactional APIs** | ≤ 1.5 seconds | N/A | Synchronous endpoints (excluding 3rd party PSP). || **Search/Lookup (Games-Time)** | ≤ 2.0 seconds | N/A | Accreditation lookups at provincial hubs. |  
**Technical Continuity Mandates (RFP 7.6)**  To protect financial and participant data integrity, the system must achieve a  **Transactional Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of ≤ 5 minutes** . The  **Recovery Time Objective (RTO)**  is mandated at  **≤ 60 minutes**  under normal conditions and must be sharpened to  **≤ 30 minutes**  during Protected and Games-Time Windows for any regional or site-level service failure.These hardware and performance guardrails facilitate a robust framework for human-centric interaction through the multi-portal architecture.

##### 3\. Multi-Portal Persona Architecture and RBAC

Strategic necessity dictates a role-based, multi-portal interface to manage diverse stakeholder needs while enforcing strict data isolation.

* **Participant Portal:**  A mobile-optimized, WCAG 2.2 Level AA compliant interface. To minimize helpdesk volume during the high-pressure 17 September launch, the architecture should support  **passwordless authentication**  (PLT-03) alongside traditional secure account recovery.  
* **Helpdesk Operator Portal:**  An interface for assisted registration. It is an absolute requirement to capture  **recorded participant authority/consent**  for every action. Audit logs must achieve total transparency, distinguishing operator-led modifications from participant self-service actions.  
* **Evidence Reviewer Portal:**  A secure queue for sport-specific administrators to verify date-of-birth ID, federation licenses, and medical certificates against OC-defined rejection codes.  
* **Sport-Specific Admin Portal:**  Dashboards for real-time entry monitoring, waitlist release management, and heat/field mapping.  
* **Finance Admin Portal:**  Access to settlement reports, VAT data exports (FatturaPA/SDI), and dual-control refund workflows.  
* **Accreditation Agent Portal:**  High-performance search and offline-fallback lookup packs for credential issuance in Como, Lecco, and Varese.Security is governed by  **Privacy-by-Design**  controls. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all privileged users. Per requirement  **PRV-04** , medical and disability data must be logically isolated using separate encryption keys and restricted access logs, preventing commingling with general profile data. These secure access layers are the prerequisite for executing the complex business logic of the Games.

##### 4\. Masters-Specific Sports Rules Engine (RUL Series)

Masters sport logic is characterized by multi-dimensional eligibility criteria that cannot be managed by static code. The solution requires a configurable, versioned rules engine where all age-based categories are calculated relative to the reference date of  **31 December 2027** .**Classification Patterns to Support**| Pattern | Required System Behaviour || \------ | \------ || **Youngest-Member Rule** | Category determined by the youngest player; real-time revalidation on roster change (Basketball, Floorball, Tennis). || **Crew Average Age** | Mean age calculation; must support exclusion of specific roles (e.g., coxswain in Rowing) and decimal rounding. || **Relay Aggregate Age** | Banding determined by the combined age sum of all four members (Swimming). || **Multi-Dimensional Matrices** | Validation of age, weight, and minimum proficiency (e.g., Judo 1st Kyu requirement). || **Handicap (Golf)** | Capture of issuing authority, index value, and verification status for Stableford formats. || **Proficiency Grades** | Support for competitive vs. recreational self-selection with sport-admin regrading (Badminton, Tennis). || **Para-Sport Classes** | Secure capture of S1-S13 classes with restricted access to underlying medical evidence. |  
**Data Model Synthesis: Entrant Lineage**  Requirement  **CMP-03**  (Category Combination) presents a major data-model challenge. To preserve competitive integrity, the database must maintain  **Entrant Lineage** . When the OC merges heats due to low entry numbers, the system must store both the original award category and the combined field mapping. This ensures that medal rankings remain tied to the athlete's original age band despite competing in a combined heat.The validation of these complex rules is triggered by the submission and verification of digital evidence.

##### 5\. Evidence Management and Team Roster Lifecycle (EVD & TEA Series)

Automated evidence validation is the primary lever for reducing OC administrative overhead. All uploads (EVD-01 to EVD-04) must undergo automated malware scanning before entering the document lifecycle. Rejection reason codes must be programmatically issued to participants to facilitate rapid, self-service resubmission without manual helpdesk intervention.The team journey (TEA-01 to TEA-04) utilizes a captain-led lifecycle:

1. **Creation/Invitation:**  Captains generate secure join links or team-specific codes.  
2. **Payment:**  The system must support split payments, team deposits, or corporate sponsorship.  
3. **Roster Lock:**  A hard temporal constraint after which rosters are frozen for competition scheduling.**Controlled Substitutions**  represent a critical real-time processing requirement. Any substitution requested after the Roster Lock must trigger an immediate recalculation of fees, eligibility categories, and accreditation entitlements to prevent category-slippage or unauthorized fee bypass. Once verified, this data flows into the phased long-term roadmap.

##### 6\. System Extensibility and Phased Roadmap (Phases A–F)

The system adheres to an "API-first" philosophy to ensure the Phase A core can integrate with future timing hardware and results systems.

* **Phase A: Core Registration (Launch 17 Sept 2026):**  Mandatory identity, sport selection, and payment orchestration.  
* **Phase B: Stabilization (Operations-Ready Gate):**  Migration of 15,000 EOI records and deduplication logic.  
* **Phase C: Competition Readiness:**  Venue mapping and results/timing interface stabilization.  
* **Phase D: Operational Readiness:**  High-load rehearsals and finalization of technical runbooks.  
* **Phase E: Games-Time Support:**  24/7 Command operations (17–26 September 2027).  
* **Phase F: Post-Event Closure:**  Data export and the issuance of  **Data Deletion Certificates** .The Phase F certificate is a strategic compliance requirement. It provides documented proof that all sensitive participant data—specifically medical evidence and ID documents—has been purged or anonymized following the legal retention period, satisfying GDPR and Italian data protection mandates.
