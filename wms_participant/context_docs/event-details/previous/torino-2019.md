**Theme / hero line:** Celebrate Life, Friendship and Sport

The European Masters Games 2019 in Torino, Italy, was a dynamic event that brought together athletes from all over Europe to celebrate the joy of sport and the spirit of friendship. With the theme “Celebrate Life, Friendship, and Sport,” the event highlighted the power of sport to foster connections and inspire a healthy, active lifestyle for all ages.

## Athlete comments

**“Sport keeps your mind active”**

“Sport keeps you fit, keeps your mind active, all those sort of things. It gives you something to work towards, so I’ll do it as long as I possibly can really.”

**“Sport helps in so many aspects of life”**

“Through sport you learn self discipline, work hard on a target, on a goal you want to reach and that also helps for your career or for your work.”

**“You might compete against World Champions”**

“Masters Games, you never know who the competition is going to be until you have a few races. Quite often they’re former Olympians or World Champions in their day. You get a very broad spectrum of athletes and their abilities over their lives which is a really good thing really.”
