Sport Without Limits.

World Masters Sport is about more than just sport. We celebrate the unbreakable human spirit to achieve our potential and inspire others. We champion our community of dreamers from around the world who play sport to build friendships and connect, next to maintaining their physical and mental wellbeing. A fulfilling and happy life is possible at any age.

Hear from our athletes

From inspiring victories to unforgettable moments, our athletes say it best! The Masters journey can be a life changing experience that refuels the fire from within.

Lulu Alexandra

The Masters Games has given me opportunities to compete on a world stage with incredible competitors in beautiful locations. I’ve made lifelong international friends in my sport and having a focus on training has had huge benefits for both my mental and physical health.

 

Nick Hope

After covering Swimming at several Olympic Games, I made the decision to return to the pool myself in order to enhance my physical and mental health. I soon realised how important regularly competition is for maintaining motivation, but also making new memories as well as friends. I hope to bring my wife and our little one out to Japan, for Kansai 2027

Jim Parrett

Since I started the journey with the Masters Games since 2013 it has been some of the most marvellous experiences of my life, besides getting married and having children – It’s fantastic !
